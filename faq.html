Lessons

Making lessons.

How long does it take to make a video?

A two-minute lesson is usually ready in about two minutes. A five-minute deep dive takes around three. You can watch the scenes appear while it builds.

What topics can it explain?

Almost anything that can be drawn: science, maths, engineering, computing, economics, biology and how everyday things work. Type the topic the way you would ask a person.

What is the difference between intuitive, standard and deep dive?

Intuitive starts from an everyday picture for newcomers. Standard explains the real mechanism with the proper terms. Deep dive goes into the internals and the maths for technical viewers, while still defining every term it uses.

What happens if I do not understand part of a video?

Press “I'm lost here” (or L) while watching. You get a short answer in a few seconds, and a one-minute follow-up video about that exact moment is made for you, in the same visual style. A button takes you back to where you were.

Is it accurate?

Lessons are written to be precise, deep dives include worked numbers, and every scene is checked before it is kept. It is still AI-generated, so double-check facts before relying on them for anything important.

Can I download the videos?

Yes. Every finished lesson can be downloaded as a 1080p mp4 with narration and captions built in.

Credits and billing

Credits and plans.

What is a credit?

1 credit is 1 minute of finished video, so a 2-minute lesson uses 2 credits. An “I'm lost here” follow-up clip uses 1 credit, and quick text answers are free.

What happens after the free month?

Your lessons stay in your account. To make new ones, buy a Study Pack or choose Monthly or Semester.

Do unused credits expire?

Study Pack credits last 12 months. On Monthly, up to 30 unused credits roll over to the next month.

What if a lesson fails?

If a lesson fails on our side, its credits go straight back to your balance.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your dashboard, under Plan and billing. You keep your credits until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you have plans for teachers and schools?

Yes: Teacher, Academy, School and Institution plans with classroom rights, shared minute pools and admin tools. See school pricing.

Your account

Signing in.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Ask a question on the home page and watch it being built. To watch the finished lesson, create a free account: the lesson you asked for is waiting in it.

I already have an account. Where do I sign in?

Use Sign in. Once you have signed in on a device, the menu shows Sign in instead of Start free.

Where do I find my lessons?

In your dashboard, under My lessons. You can search them, filter by depth, watch, download or copy a share link.
