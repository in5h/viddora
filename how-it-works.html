How it works

One sentence in, a finished video out.

About two minutes from question to lesson. You can watch each step happen and the scenes appear as they are made.

About two minutes for a 2-minute lesson0 s — 115 s
~15 s

It writes the lesson

A script for the depth you picked: one idea per scene, with what the narrator says and what you will see.

~10 s

It records the narration

A natural voice reads every scene. Each word is timed, so captions and reveals land exactly when they are said.

~60 s

It animates every scene

Each scene is drawn as a real animation of the thing being explained, then a vision model checks the frame before it is kept.

~30 s

It renders your video

Scenes render in parallel at 1080p and are joined with the audio into one video you can watch or download.

Your pace, your level

The same topic, explained three ways.

A newcomer and an engineer need different lessons. Pick the depth and viddora changes how it writes, what it draws and how much maths it shows.

For newcomers

Intuitive

Starts with an everyday example, builds an easy picture and ends with a one-line recap.

“Imagine a highway where everyone floors the gas at once…”
You know the basics

Standard

The real mechanism in the order it happens, with proper terms defined as they appear and one worked example.

“The model keeps a running list of every word it has already processed. Engineers call it the cache.”
For engineers

Deep dive

Internals, real numbers and the maths: what it measures, the formula, then what the answer means.

“Three point two eight gigabytes. That is three gigabytes of memory just to remember one chat.”
Ask the video

Lost at one part? Press L.

Every lesson knows what was being said at every second. Press “I'm lost here” and choose how you want it explained: simpler, with an example, or with what you need to know first.

  1. A clear answer in about five seconds.
  2. A one-minute follow-up video about that exact moment, in the same style.
  3. One click back to the second you left.
Questions

Good to know.

How long does it take to make a video?

A two-minute lesson is usually ready in about two minutes. A five-minute deep dive takes around three. You can watch the scenes appear while it builds.

What topics can it explain?

Almost anything that can be drawn: science, maths, engineering, computing, economics, biology and how everyday things work. Type the topic the way you would ask a person.

What is the difference between intuitive, standard and deep dive?

Intuitive starts from an everyday picture for newcomers. Standard explains the real mechanism with the proper terms. Deep dive goes into the internals and the maths for technical viewers, while still defining every term it uses.

What happens if I do not understand part of a video?

Press “I'm lost here” (or L) while watching. You get a short answer in a few seconds, and a one-minute follow-up video about that exact moment is made for you, in the same visual style. A button takes you back to where you were.

Is it accurate?

Lessons are written to be precise, deep dives include worked numbers, and every scene is checked before it is kept. It is still AI-generated, so double-check facts before relying on them for anything important.

Can I download the videos?

Yes. Every finished lesson can be downloaded as a 1080p mp4 with narration and captions built in.
